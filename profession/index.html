AI Licensing & Credit System

Strategy Pricing Model

When RealLives shifted to a new AI-driven architecture, static unlimited-play pricing no longer worked, token costs meant every simulated life had a real cost behind it. I led the shift to a credit-based system (1 credit = 1 simulated life), grounded in thorough token-cost evaluation, to build a licensing model that could actually scale with the new architecture.

Global Growth, Google Ads
